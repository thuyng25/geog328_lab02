Matcha in Global Market

Many of the most respected matcha producers are still based in Japan for example, Marukyu Koyamaen (MK), a farm in Kyoto, Japan that is supplying high-quality ceremonial matcha. However, growing global demand has outpaced domestic production. As a result, more matcha is now being sourced from other regions such as China, where larger farms can scale production quickly. This expansion makes matcha more accessible worldwide, but it also raises questions about quality standards, authenticity, and preservation of traditional farming methods.
